Reef Beauty

A beauty line created as an extension of Reef’s world, translating nature into a refined, feminine product experience. Inspired by florals, light, and softness, the brand blends luxury with simplicity to create products that feel elevated yet effortless. Beyond the aesthetic, the focus was on developing formulas, selecting textures, and defining hero ingredients that deliver both quality and performance. From shade names to packaging, every detail is designed to evoke a sense of natural beauty and emotional connection; where quality is felt, and beauty blooms.

Ten Seconds
Skincare · Nice One

Ten Seconds

Built around the idea that skincare should fit into real life, not slow it down, Ten Seconds reimagines routines through speed, simplicity, and intention. Inspired by Korean beauty trends, the brand focuses on high-demand ingredients such as snail mucin, translating them into accessible, easy-to-use products designed for everyday use. Beyond the concept, the focus was on selecting formulas, refining textures, and identifying trend-driven hero ingredients that deliver visible results without complexity. The result is a skincare experience that feels effortless, current, and built for how people actually live.

One Pose

Built around the idea that beauty doesn’t need effort to feel complete, One Pose focuses on clean, everyday makeup designed for simplicity and confidence. Inspired by effortless, photo-ready beauty, the brand blends minimal aesthetics with carefully selected formulas, refined textures, and hero ingredients that deliver consistent, wearable results. The focus was on creating products that enhance rather than transform, balancing ease of use with performance, and making everyday makeup feel intuitive, reliable, and naturally elevated.
